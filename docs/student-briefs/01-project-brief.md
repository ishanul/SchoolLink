# Project Brief: SchoolLink

## Background

Schools need reliable communication between school administrators, teachers, parents, and students. In many Sri Lankan schools, this communication still happens through a mix of WhatsApp groups, printed notices, phone calls, verbal messages, and manual records.

These methods are familiar and easy to start, but they can become difficult to manage as the number of students, classes, parents, teachers, events, assignments, and notices grows.

For example:

- A parent may miss an important school notice in a busy WhatsApp group.
- A teacher may need to repeat the same information across multiple channels.
- A school administrator may struggle to track who has received or responded to a notice.
- A student may forget assignment deadlines or depend fully on parents to track work.
- Consent forms and event confirmations may still require paper forms and manual follow-up.

## Project Idea

SchoolLink is a proposed digital platform for Sri Lankan schools.

The idea is to create one structured place where schools, teachers, parents, and optionally students can access important school-related information.

The platform should help reduce scattered communication, missed updates, paper-based workflows, and unnecessary manual administration.

This project is inspired by platforms such as Singapore's Parents Gateway, but it should not be treated as a direct copy. The solution should be designed for the Sri Lankan school context.

## Target Users

The platform may support the following users:

- School administrators
- Teachers
- Parents or guardians
- Students, with limited access if appropriate

Each user type has different needs. For example, school administrators may care about official notices and records, while parents may care about updates related to their own children.

## Main Problem Areas

The project should explore how a digital platform can improve areas such as:

- School announcements
- Class-level communication
- Assignment and homework visibility
- Digital consent or permission forms
- School events and reminders
- Parent awareness of important deadlines
- Teacher and administrator workload
- Student accountability

The project does not need to solve all of these problems at once. Part of the student group's work is to understand the problem and decide what should be handled first.

## Local Context

The solution should consider real conditions in Sri Lankan schools, such as:

- Different levels of digital literacy among parents
- Use of Sinhala, Tamil, and English
- Low-end Android devices
- Uneven internet access
- Schools with limited administrative staff
- Parents with multiple children in the same or different schools
- Students with more than one guardian
- The need to protect student and family information

## Product Name

The current working name is SchoolLink.

The student group may keep this name or propose a better name. Any proposed name should be suitable for schools, teachers, parents, and students in Sri Lanka.

## What Students Should Think About

Before designing or building the system, students should think carefully about:

- What problems are most important to solve first?
- Who are the main users?
- What information should each user be allowed to see?
- How can the platform reduce teacher workload instead of increasing it?
- How can the platform avoid sending too many notifications?
- How should the system handle consent forms and parent responses?
- How can students remain accountable for their own work?
- What privacy and security risks exist when handling student data?

## Goal Of The Project

The goal is to design a practical, useful, and locally relevant school communication platform.

The project should help students practice:

- Understanding real user problems
- Defining a focused first version
- Writing clear requirements
- Designing user roles and workflows
- Thinking about data, privacy, and security
- Building software that fits a real-world context
