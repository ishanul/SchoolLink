# Task: Data Model and Wireframes

## Goal

Create early design artifacts that show how the system may work.

These do not need to be perfect. They should help the group discuss and improve the proposed solution.

## Data Model

Identify the main data entities and relationships needed for your proposed MVP.

Do not begin by copying a suggested list of tables. Think from your requirements and workflows.

Ask:

- What information must the system store?
- Which users are connected to which records?
- What relationships are one-to-one, one-to-many, or many-to-many?
- What data should be private?
- What data should be auditable?

Your model should consider:

- A parent or guardian may have more than one child.
- A student may have more than one guardian.
- A teacher may be connected to more than one class or subject.
- A school may have many classes, students, teachers, and parents.

## Wireframes

Create low-fidelity wireframes for the main screens in your proposed MVP.

For each screen, show:

- Main purpose of the screen
- Important information shown
- Main actions available to the user
- Any important empty, error, or confirmation states

## Expected Output

- ER diagram or entity relationship explanation
- Short explanation of important relationships
- Low-fidelity wireframes
- Short explanation of each wireframe
- Open questions or design concerns
